# Board Pack Template, the executive standard

The task generates the pack in this exact shape. Ordering matters: what the board must act on comes first, the evidence after. Keep it to roughly two screens. A board pack is denser than the engineering reports, but it is still read fastest when it leads with what matters.

Replace bracketed guidance with real content at generation time. Do not print the guidance.

---

[Three or four sentences. Lead with the headline financials: revenue, burn, runway, each with its month-over-month direction and standing versus plan. Then the overall OKR posture for the quarter to date. Then the single most important thing the board should know this month. If everything is on plan and on track, say so plainly.]

## Needs board attention

[The short list of things that need the board to know or act, per the escalation lens in board_metrics.md: objectives off track or key results that slipped a bucket, metrics off plan beyond the band or with a material month-over-month move, and runway below the floor. If nothing qualifies, write "Nothing needs board attention this month" and move on.]
